# PermitKeep launch campaign

## Current status

The static product and original owned-search guide are prepared for public GitHub Pages deployment. The sale, checkout, and promotional launch remain gated until a product-specific live Stripe product, one-time price, and payment link are created and read back.

## Positioning

**See every permit and renewal deadline without uploading the ledger to another business system.** PermitKeep gives small multi-location operators a calm local-first dashboard, explicit owners, portable backup, CSV, and calendar exports—without guessing requirements or pretending to monitor agencies.

## Claims allowed

- Works in a modern browser and can be installed as a PWA.
- Permit records stay in the browser during normal use; the app has no record database, analytics, ads, external scripts, or account system.
- Renewal-start dates are calculated from dates and lead windows entered by the operator.
- Backup, CSV, and calendar exports are generated locally.
- The application does not scrape, monitor, or infer government requirements.

## Claims prohibited

Do not call PermitKeep compliance software, an official agency service, encrypted storage, automatic monitoring, a filing service, or a guarantee against expiration, fines, closure, or data loss. Do not claim customers, sales, savings, agency endorsement, legal review, trademark clearance, or results without evidence.

## Initial lawful channels

1. Public source repository and GitHub Pages product page.
2. Original owned guide: `permit-renewal-tracking-checklist.html`.
3. Search discovery through `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and a compliant IndexNow submission.
4. Product directories that allow maker submissions without fabricated traction.
5. Useful, affiliation-disclosed participation only in communities whose rules permit self-promotion.
6. Opt-in outreach to accountants, franchise consultants, chambers, and local-business educators; no scraped lists or bulk unsolicited messaging.

## Launch evidence standard

For each external write, record the exact URL, timestamp, account, copy, maker disclosure, rules checked, destination URL, and readback result. Record a sale only after the live Stripe payment is read back as succeeded. Start with owned surfaces, then one small compliant directory/community batch after checkout verification.
