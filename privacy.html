PKPermitKeepPrivacy and data custody

Plain-language privacy notice · Effective 2026-09-02

Your deadline ledger stays with you.

PermitKeep is designed as a static, local-first browser app. It does not require an account and the app code does not send your permit records to a PermitKeep server.

What is stored

Records you enter are saved in this browser's local storage. A record can include permit names and numbers, locations, issuing agencies, owners, dates, URLs, and notes. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or changing devices can erase or strand those records.

What is not included

This release contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts, third-party scripts, cloud sync, or automated agency monitoring. The app does not request card data, government credentials, or identity documents.

Exports and outside links

Backup, CSV, and calendar files are generated on your device. Once exported, you control where those files go. An official-agency link you add can navigate to that external site; its own privacy practices apply.

Security boundary

Local storage is not a secure vault and is not encrypted by PermitKeep. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to read the records. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, Social Security numbers, tax IDs, or other secrets. Use your device's account security and disk encryption, and keep backups appropriately protected.

Operational boundary

PermitKeep organizes user-entered facts. It does not determine required permits, monitor changes in law, verify agency data, file renewals, or provide legal, tax, or compliance advice. Confirm all requirements and deadlines with the issuing authority.
